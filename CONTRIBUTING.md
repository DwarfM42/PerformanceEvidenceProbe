# Contributing

Please open an issue or pull request with a focused change and tests or other
reproducible evidence appropriate to the change. Do not submit target programs,
private evidence bundles, credentials, or material you are not authorized to
share.

## Inbound license

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you represent that you are
entitled to submit it and, unless you explicitly identify different terms that
the maintainers accept in writing, you license your contribution under the
project's outbound license terms: **MIT OR Apache-2.0**, at the recipient's
option. This permits the project and downstream recipients to use the accepted
contribution under either license.

No contributor license agreement is required. This policy does not transfer
copyright ownership or grant a right to relicense a third-party contribution
under arbitrary proprietary terms. A later proprietary relicensing of code
owned by another contributor would require separate permission from the
relevant rightsholder.
